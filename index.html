Yifang (Mark) Chen

Multimodal models · Structured data · Reasoning

Hi! I am Yifang (Mark) Chen (陈义方), a rising senior at New York University Shanghai, majoring in Computer Science with minors in Mathematics and Data Science.

I am currently a Research Assistant at the Data Intelligence and Reasoning Lab at NYU Shanghai, advised by Prof. Qiaoyu Tan. I am also fortunate to be co-advised by Prof. Jinyang Li on my research project.

My research interests lie at the intersection of multimodal models, graph-structured data, and reasoning.
